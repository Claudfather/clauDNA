# Design Hard Rules


Reference material for the `/claudna:audit design` lens. These rules are used by the page-type classifier in Step 2 and inform the gap analysis in Step 4. They are reference material — do NOT display these tables to the user. Use them to calibrate your evaluation.

## Hard Rejection Criteria

Instant-fail patterns. If ANY apply, flag as a High-impact finding in the gap analysis.

1. Generic SaaS card grid as first impression
2. Beautiful image with weak or absent brand
3. Strong headline with no clear action
4. Busy imagery behind text (unreadable)
5. Sections repeating the same mood statement
6. Carousel with no narrative purpose
7. App UI made of stacked cards instead of structured layout

## Litmus Checks

Answer YES/NO for each audited page. Record the results in the gap analysis research file. Pages scoring less than 4/7 YES answers warrant at least one High-impact finding.

1. Brand/product unmistakable in the first screen?
2. One strong visual anchor present?
3. Page understandable by scanning headlines only?
4. Each section has one job?
5. Are cards actually necessary (or decorative)?
6. Does motion improve hierarchy or atmosphere?
7. Would the design feel premium with all decorative shadows removed?

## Page-Type Specific Rules

### Marketing / Landing Pages

- First viewport reads as one composition (poster, not document)
- Brand-first hierarchy: brand > headline > body > CTA
- Typography should be expressive and purposeful
- Hero: full-bleed, not inset or tiled
- One job per section: one purpose, one headline, one supporting sentence
- Motion: 2-3 intentional animations minimum (entrance, scroll-linked, hover)

### App UI

- Calm surface hierarchy, strong typography, few colors
- Dense but readable, minimal chrome
- Organize: primary workspace, navigation, secondary context, one accent
- Avoid: dashboard-card mosaics, thick borders, decorative gradients
- Copy: utility language — orientation, status, action (not mood or aspiration)
- Cards only when card IS the interaction
